# Project Library Overview

This library provides a series of **progressive, project‑based learning specifications** for the Software Engineering Academy.  Each project is mapped to the weekly syllabus, introduces increasingly complex engineering concepts, and includes comprehensive documentation requirements and assessment rubrics.

## Projects
- **[Project 1 – Personal Finance CLI](project-1-spec.md)** – Beginner, weeks 2‑4
- **[Project 2 – Inventory Management Application](project-2-spec.md)** – Intermediate, weeks 5‑8
- **[Project 3 – Multi‑user Inventory API](project-3-spec.md)** – Intermediate‑Advanced, weeks 9‑12
- **[Project 4 – Full‑stack Business Operations Platform](project-4-spec.md)** – Advanced, weeks 13‑16
- **[Project 5 – Production Engineering Upgrade](project-5-spec.md)** – Advanced, weeks 17‑20
- **[Capstone – Warehouse and Logistics Operations Management Platform](capstone-spec.md)** – Final project, weeks 21‑24

## Critical‑Thinking Scenarios
- **[Engineering Challenges](challenges.md)** – Situational problems that require analysis, design decisions, and trade‑off evaluation.  Solutions are provided in the **Reference Solutions** folder for instructors.

## Documentation & Assessment
- **[Project Documentation Requirements](documentation-requirements.md)** – Checklist of mandatory artefacts for every project.
- **[Project Assessment Rubric](assessment-rubric.md)** – Grading criteria used for all projects and the capstone.

---
*All specifications are intentionally **incomplete** – learners must fill in designs, code, and tests themselves before consulting the reference solutions.*
